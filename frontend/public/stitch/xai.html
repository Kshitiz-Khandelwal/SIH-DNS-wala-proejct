Shannon Information Entropy

H(X)

Measures the randomness of character distributions across the FQDN label string. Benign English words score <3.5; algorithmic pseudo-random DGAs score >4.2 bits.

H(X) = - ∑_{i=1}^n P(x_i) · log₂( P(x_i) )

Bi-gram Perplexity & Language Probability

PP(W)

Quantifies the probability of character transition sequences under natural English corpus frequencies. Low transition probability indicates synthetic Generation.

PP(W) = P(w_1, w_2, ..., w_N)^(-1/N) = 2^( H(W) )

Consonant-to-Vowel Ratio & Consecutive Length

R_cv

Analyzes phonetic pronounceability. Algorithmic domains (e.g. `xk9mqz`) exhibit extreme consonant clustering exceeding 75% total character mass.

R_cv = N_consonants / ( N_vowels + 1 ); Max_Cons_Streak ≥ 5

Lundberg & Lee Exact TreeSHAP Formulation

φᵢ(f,x)

Computes exact marginal Shapley contributions across all 150 ensemble trees in polynomial O(TLD²) time without sampling approximations.

φᵢ = ∑_{S ⊆ F\{i}} [|S|!(|F|-|S|-1)! / |F|!] · [f_x(S ∪ {i}) - f_x(S)]
