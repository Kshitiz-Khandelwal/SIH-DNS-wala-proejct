Why Random Forest & XGBoost Over Deep Neural Networks?

Why Unicode Skeleton & Jaro-Winkler for Typosquatting?

Standard Levenshtein distance fails on homoglyphs. Our approach normalizes strings before distance calculation.

Why Markov Transition Chains for DNS Tunnelling?

Statistical modeling of character transitions is highly effective at identifying the high-entropy nature of base64/hex payloads.
